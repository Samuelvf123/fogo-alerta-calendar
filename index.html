Methods, data and credits

Data

  • NASA FIRMS country yearly summaries (standard processing): MODIS Collection 6.1 active fires (Terra and Aqua, 1 km, from Nov 2000) and VIIRS 375 m active fires from NOAA-20 (from 2018). Only detections typed as presumed vegetation fire (type 0) are counted, so static sources such as gas flares are excluded.
  • Region: 10°S–2.5°N, 74°W–56°W (Amazonas state and surroundings), aggregated into 0.25° cells (about 28 km).
  • Base map © OpenStreetMap contributors. Libraries: Leaflet, Chart.js.

Harmonization

  1. VIIRS (NOAA-20) is the reference, because it continues after MODIS ends and sees more detail.
  2. In the overlap years (2018–2025), for each calendar month and for day and night passes, we compute a factor k = VIIRS hotspots ÷ MODIS hotspots.
  3. Before 2018, VIIRS-equivalent hotspots = k × MODIS hotspots (day and night separately). From 2018 on, observed VIIRS counts are used. Before Aqua started (July 2002) only Terra was in orbit, so Terra-only factors are used for those months.
  4. Validation: we hide one year, fit the factors without it, predict it from MODIS and compare with the real VIIRS counts.

Limits

  • Counts are hotspots (fire pixels), not number of fires or burned area.
  • Clouds and smoke hide fires; the rainy season has fewer valid observations.
  • One region-wide factor per month and pass; local differences in fire size are not modeled yet.
  • Terra and Aqua orbits drifted after 2020; refitting the factors with 2018–2019 only changes the pre-2018 totals by less than 1% (see Validation).
  • 2026 standard-processing data were not yet published when this was built.
